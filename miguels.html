What we do

Services built for southern Utah yards

Hard water, clay soil, and 110° summers are rough on a landscape. Every service below is priced flat and scheduled on a route, so you know who is coming and when.

Weekly Lawn Care

Mow, edge, string-trim, and blow down every visit. We haul the clippings and adjust cut height through the season so the turf holds color into July.

  • • Same crew, same day each week
  • • Fertilization add-on available

Sprinkler Repair

Broken heads, cracked laterals, dead valves, and controllers that never got programmed right. We find the problem and fix it the same visit when parts allow.

  • • Leak & valve diagnostics
  • • Smart timer setup for water rules
Get pricing →

Desert Cleanups

Overgrown xeriscape reset: weed removal, gravel refresh, palm and shrub trimming, pruning back sage and oleander, and full debris haul-off.

  • • One-time or seasonal
  • • Pre-emergent weed treatment

Sod Installation

Grade, amend, and lay heat-tolerant sod cut fresh from the farm — plus a watering schedule that gets roots down before the first heat wave.
